This policy explains how ShutterNote, a film photography companion app, handles camera, photo, settings and distance measurement data.

1. Camera and sensors

The camera is used for manual exposure previews, photography and measuring distance to the central subject. The rangefinder uses camera and motion sensor information through Google Play Services for AR (ARCore). Denying camera permission disables the relevant features. You can change this permission in device settings.

2. Photo processing and storage

Exposure adjustments are processed on your device. The app itself does not upload captured photos to a developer server. Tapping Save stores the result in the CameraOption album on your device. Filenames include capture settings and the capture time.

Temporary photos are kept in the app cache to restore captured results. Files older than 24 hours are cleaned up the next time you open the main screen or exposure camera. Cleanup may be delayed if you do not reopen the app, and the operating system may clear the cache sooner. Saved gallery photos are not included in this cleanup.

3. Settings and backups

The app stores exposure values, field of view, language, ARCore notice acknowledgement and save status to prevent duplicate saves on your device. Android backup is currently enabled, so settings and save status may be backed up and restored depending on your device and backup service. Temporary photos in the app cache are excluded from Android Auto Backup.

The app itself has no account registration, advertising SDK or separate user analytics service.

4. Google Play Services for AR

Distance measurement uses ARCore, provided by Google and governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Google states that ARCore collects and processes account or device identifiers, performance and diagnostic information, API usage and app activity for analytics and service improvements. Data sent to Google is encrypted using HTTPS.

This app does not use ARCore Cloud Anchors or the Geospatial API. You can use the exposure camera and photo saving without using the rangefinder. See the Google documents below for details about ARCore data processing and retention.

5. Deletion and control

Clearing app data in device settings or uninstalling the app removes its internally stored settings and data. Delete saved photos separately in your gallery. Manage any copies held by device or photo backup services through those services. ARCore data processed by Google is subject to Google policies and account controls.

6. This website

This page contains no added advertising or visitor analytics scripts. The hosting service may process access and security information, such as IP addresses, to deliver the page. External links are subject to the privacy policies of those services.

7. Changes

We update this policy and its document date when features or data practices change. We also provide in-app notice when needed.
